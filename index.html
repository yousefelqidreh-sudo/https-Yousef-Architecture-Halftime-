01 / THE PROJECT
HALFTIME — LOT 11

One building.
Two rhythms.

An urban mixed-use concept that places active commercial spaces along the public edge while keeping residential access and circulation independent.

Developed for Algonquin College's empty lot 11, the proposal explores how a compact building can balance street presence, privacy, environmental response, and technical detailing.

02 / SITE RESPONSE
CONTEXT + ENVIRONMENT

Designed for
its setting

Lot 11 is part of the Algonquin College campus in Ottawa. The site was planned around how people arrive and move through it, with landscaping that makes it feel welcoming and works through Ottawa’s changing seasons.
